Full-payment proposals require an exact amount, the same property account, an entirely unpaid obligation, and a unique candidate for this month. Memo text is shown for your review; the app does not infer payer identity.

Running matching saves proposals and unresolved issues. It does not record a payment. Rejected or withdrawn proposals are retained and are not automatically recreated.
