CSV format and duplicate rules

Required columns: transaction_id,posted_date,description,amount. Each row needs an identifier, a YYYY-MM-DD posted date, a description, and a positive USD amount with at most two decimal places.

Configured property phrases are matched as case-insensitive substrings of the description. One matching property is assigned; no matches or matches to multiple properties require manual assignment below. Multiple matching phrases for the same property count as one match. Amount is not used to identify a property.

Transaction IDs must be unique across this feed, including across properties. Identical files and repeated IDs with identical dates, descriptions, and amounts are skipped. Conflicting details reject the entire file. Label changes never reassign imported transactions.
