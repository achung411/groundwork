Account activity

Oldest first. Rent charges increase the balance on their due dates; approved payments reduce it on their received dates. The bank posted date is used as the received date. Charges appear before payments on the same day. A negative balance is a credit, such as rent received before its due date.

Ledger coverage and date rules

USD base rent only. This ledger includes saved rent obligations, not automatically calculated charges for every lease month. It starts at zero before the first saved entry; earlier balances, fees, and other charges are not included. The selected date filters due and posted dates using current approvals, rather than reconstructing what was known at that time.
