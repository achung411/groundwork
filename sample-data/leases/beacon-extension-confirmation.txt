2. EXERCISE CONFIRMATION

For this fictional scenario, the parties confirm that written notice exercising the lease's sole three-year extension option was delivered and accepted on September 15, 2026. 3. RENT DURING EXTENSION

This sample does not establish rent during the extension. Do not assume that the current base rent carries forward. Approval of an exercise event does not establish a new rent obligation.
