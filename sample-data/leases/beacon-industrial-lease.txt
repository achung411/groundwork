1. PARTIES, PREMISES, AND TERM

This Commercial Lease is dated August 1, 2022, between Blue Lantern Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Tidepool Printworks Inc. ("Tenant") for Unit B at Beacon Industrial, a fictional commercial property located at 88 Beacon Yard, Fairhaven, Oregon.

The term begins September 1, 2022 and ends August 31, 2027, unless ended earlier under this Lease.

2. MONTHLY RENT

Tenant will pay monthly base rent of $3,200.00, due on the fifth calendar day of each month.

3. GRACE PERIOD

Payment received within five calendar days after the due date is within the grace period. Under this clause, payment received on the fifth day after the due date remains within the grace period; payment received later than that is past the grace period. This clause does not state a late fee.

4. EXTENSION OPTION AND NOTICE

Tenant may exercise one option to extend the term for three years. Written notice must be delivered no earlier than twelve months and no later than nine months before the current expiration date. The option has not been exercised in this sample.

5. OTHER CHARGES

Tenant is responsible for the NNN expenses allocated under this Lease. This sample does not include a statement of those expenses.
