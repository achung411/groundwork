FICTIONAL COMMERCIAL LEASE — ORCHARD ROW RETAIL

All parties, names, terms, and amounts in this sample are invented for demonstration. This is not a real lease or legal form.

1. PARTIES AND PREMISES

This Commercial Lease is dated December 15, 2023, between Cedar Lantern Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Willow & Pine Outfitters LLC ("Tenant"). The leased premises are Suite 104 at Orchard Row Retail, a fictional commercial property located at 14 Orchard Row, Fairhaven, Oregon.

2. TERM

The initial term begins January 1, 2024 and ends December 31, 2028, unless ended earlier under a provision of this Lease.

--- Page 2 ---

3. BASE RENT AND PAYMENT

Tenant will pay monthly base rent of $4,800.00. Base rent is due in advance on the first calendar day of each month. Payment must be delivered to the account or address later designated in writing by Landlord.

4. ADDITIONAL RENT

Tenant is responsible for the operating expenses allocated to Tenant under the NNN provisions in this Lease. This sample does not specify or calculate those expenses.

--- Page 3 ---

5. EXTENSION OPTION

Tenant may exercise one option to extend the term for five years. Tenant must give written notice no earlier than twelve months and no later than nine months before the then-current expiration date. The parties will determine base rent for the extension in a signed writing. This Lease does not state a fixed extension rent amount.

6. LATE PAYMENT

This Lease states the monthly due date but does not provide a grace period or a late fee. Do not infer either term from this sample.
